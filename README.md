# Garnet - PSP & SaaS Application

A **Payment Service Provider (PSP) SaaS** platform, simplifying accepting paymens online, designed to make your revenue grow. Platform providers you with a large variety of services and third-party connections, everything to manage your business using Garnet.

***NOTE*** that this app is simply a project, it's not connected to real gateways & nor does it offer financial support.

## Key Features

Garnet enables businesses to accept and manage online payments without having to build and maintain a payment infrastructure from scratch.

### Payment Processing

- Secure payment initiation
- Multiple supported payment methods and currencies
- Payment status tracking & analytics
- Refund support for cancellation

### Merchant Related

- Merchant onboarding
- Profiles and configuration
- Customized settings
- Services making analytics better

### Transactions

- Transaction tracking, searching and filtering
- Payment status updates
- Detailed transaction records
- Webhook based event notifications

### Payouts & Settlements

- Automated merchant payouts
- Settlement tracking
- Payout history
- Configurable settlemend schedules

### Hardware

- Payments can be accepted locally
- Every type of hardware for your staff and customers

### Funding & Support

- Get up to $25,000 dollars in support, whatever it takes to get your first business up and running
